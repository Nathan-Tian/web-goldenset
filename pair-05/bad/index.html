Prologue

The state of our home

The Earth has been our home for 200,000 years of human memory — and for 3.8 billion years, it has done something no other place in the universe is known to do: it holds life.

We are the first generation to see the full picture, and the last with a real chance to change the ending. This page is a journey: from the beauty that still surrounds us, through the quiet alarms, to the practical, joyful work of bringing the world back into balance.
